Slate
Games are completed in the sims before they appear here. Results are hidden until you settle — you pick blind.
Points
You get 1,000 points for the whole season. Each pick costs 20 points (50 picks total). The bankroll carries across weeks — spend it wisely.
Score
Each game has a random score value (7,499 – 11,205) set when the slate is built. Correct picks earn that score. Wrong picks earn nothing.
